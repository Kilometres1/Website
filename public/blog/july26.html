The Isle of May

I visited the Isle of May National Nature Reserve! It is a gorgeous little island located off the scottish coast, near Edinburgh. It is home to thousands of seabirds, including Puffins, Shag, Razorbills, and many more.

We took an ~hour long boat trip to get there and back, and spent about 3 hours walking around and taking pictures. The water around the island was this incredible turquoise shade and looked amazing.

I think this was the first time I've ever seen a puffin and ohhh my godddd these things are adorable askdjhasd <3333

New Camera

Last week I came across an old Soviet Zorki 4 in an antiques centre, which looked to be in pretty good condition! I believe it was made in 1973 (Serial Number: #73002181). I've since taken it home, cleaned it up, and fixed the stuck shutter curtain and speed dial.

L-Band Weather Station

As part of the plans for an L-Band recieving station, I need a lighter (and ideally bigger) dish than the existing 1m steel offset dish.

The plan is to make an aluminium mesh dish, with a diameter of 1.5m.

Big progress is being made on the 1.5m satellite dish, later this month/start of august I'll finish it and testing will begin. This dish should be big enough to overcome any losses from imperfect shape (this is replacing a 1m dish so an ideal collecting power increase of 2.25x).

still need to add the feed, then find a way to mount it; stay tuned!

The dish is basically done, it just needs an adjustment to the feed position, and the mounting system still isn't present.

I'll see what I can do for the rotator, because the dish is really hard to optimise when I'm holding the damn thing up the whole time.
